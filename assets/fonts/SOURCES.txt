Bundled font sources (downloaded from the official Google Fonts repository).

Instrument Serif: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/instrumentserif
Inter Tight: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/intertight
Geist Mono: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/geistmono
Manrope: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/manrope
DM Serif Display: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/dmserifdisplay

Each family is distributed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. The original
OFL files are included beside these fonts and packaged with the application.
Inter Tight and Geist Mono are the upstream variable-weight font files.
Manrope is the upstream variable-weight font file (200-800); DM Serif Display
includes the upstream regular and italic files. Added for Forest atelier on
2026-10-02 with their unmodified OFL license files.
